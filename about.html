About LawMate.AI

LawMate.AI uses optimized Nemotron AI to scan and explain legal documents to make sure you don't sign anything you don't fully understand.

Multilingual Legal Intelligence

You may not understand documents in a different language, but our system has a translation tool to help explain the document's clauses to you in YOUR language.

Our software flags likely risks and compiles it into a summary to help you understand possible problems before making any agreements.

The Architecture
  • Client: Mobile camera captures & PDF uploads.
  • Database: Supabase bucket storage & metadata logs.
  • Intelligence: Nemotron API used to analyze document & do risk classification.
